Place

Selected

G move · R turn · S size, then move the mouse and click (Esc cancels). X/Y lock a move to an axis; type a number for an exact value; Shift is fine, Ctrl snaps. Or drag the handles on the object: arrows move, the ring turns, the corner box sizes.

Click to place, drag to turn. Shift-click one to remove it. Baked into the terrain: it blocks walking and casts shadows.

Place puts scenery down from a model. For a placeable with settings (a chest that holds items, a lever with a script), use Things, which places one from its blueprint.

Scatter

Click models to add them to the mix (click again to take one out). Drag to scatter; Shift-drag to erase.

Things

Properties

Ramp

Drag from one level to another. The brush size sets the width (at least 6 m: narrower passages defeat NWN's pathfinding). It rises to where you let go, unless you set a rise.

Descent

In a cave or interior, drag a box over whole tiles. It is filled with halls side by side, each lower than the last, joined by a ramp at alternating ends. Each floor takes two tiles across the box; the halls run along its longer side.

Entrances

Click a slope (or a cave wall) to carve one; Shift-click one to remove it. Each has a door: pick where it leads (an entrance door in the same module), or link it in the toolset.

Textures

No engine: sculpting only. The Landscaper engine (landscaper serve) isn't running, so importing, exporting, the path check, the texture library and the game's models and blueprints are off. Sculpting works, and the browser keeps your area. How to start the engine
Left-drag to sculpt or paint · right-drag to turn · middle-drag to move · wheel to zoom · arrows move, Shift+arrows turn, +/- zoom, Home top-down, F frame
