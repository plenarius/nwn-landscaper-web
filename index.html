Place

Selected

G move · R turn · S size, then move the mouse and click (Esc cancels). X/Y lock a move to an axis; type a number for an exact value; Shift is fine, Ctrl snaps. Or drag the handles on the object: arrows move, the ring turns, the corner box sizes.

Click to place, drag to turn. Shift-click one to remove it. Baked into the terrain: it blocks walking and casts shadows.

Place puts scenery down from a model. For a placeable with settings (a chest that holds items, a lever with a script), use Things, which places one from its blueprint.

Scatter

Click models to add them to the mix (click again to take one out). Drag to scatter; Shift-drag to erase.

Ambience

Particles the export bakes into the tiles: mist, spray, fireflies, leaves, motes, light shafts, blowing sand and snow, embers, ground fog. Click a kind for the brush: drag to add it, Shift-drag to clear it. Fill puts the ticked kinds where the area calls for them, in place of the ones they had.

Night-only kinds (fireflies) show when the view shows the area by night (Sky & fog); while this panel is open, those out of their hours show as grey dots. The view sketches where particles go; the game draws the pack's own.

Things

Properties

Chasm

Drag to open a bottomless chasm: the ground drops away into the dark, and nobody can walk into it or across it, as with a cliff or deep water. Shift-drag fills it in again, the ground as it was. For outdoor areas.

A chasm on the area's edge carries on beyond it, so a plateau or an island seems to stand over nothing. To cross one, leave a strip of ground across it (Shift-drag one back): a land bridge at least 6 m wide, over the middle of a tile's edge, where NWN's pathfinding crosses from tile to tile.

Light

Drag to give the tiles under the brush these two lights; Shift-drag turns them off. Each tile has two, over its middle, reaching 14 m: a broad wash of colour (warm pools in a village at night, a cold glow in a cave), not a lamp. For a lamp or a torch, place a placeable that gives light.

Looks (outlined: for this kind of area)

Colours

The game lights with them by day too: outdoors by day, leave them off. The view shows them under the area's light (Sky & fog, Show in the view).

Ramp

Drag from one level to another. The brush size sets the width (at least 6 m: narrower passages defeat NWN's pathfinding). It rises to where you let go, unless you set a rise.

Descent

In a cave or interior, drag a box over whole tiles. It is filled with halls side by side, each lower than the last, joined by a ramp at alternating ends. Each floor takes two tiles across the box; the halls run along its longer side.

Entrances

Click a slope (or a cave wall) to carve one; Shift-click one to remove it. Each has a door: pick where it leads (an entrance door in the same module), or link it in the toolset.

Textures

Click textures to put them in or take them out (Shift-pick adds one from the ground). Cover: how much ground it paints.

        This browser isn't giving the page 3D graphics (WebGL)
        
            
        Common on remote desktops without a GPU. Either turn on hardware acceleration
            (chrome://settings/system, or brave://settings/system in Brave, then relaunch),
            or start a separate window with software rendering:
        
            
        google-chrome --user-data-dir=/tmp/landscaper-browser --enable-unsafe-swiftshader --ignore-gpu-blocklist URL
        
        
        
        
        
            
        The app's window isn't getting 3D graphics (WebGL)
        
            
        On Linux, start the app with LANDSCAPER_WEBVIEW_SAFE=1 set, which turns off the parts of the window's
            drawing that fail with some graphics drivers. If it still shows nothing, start the program alone (the release's
            archive for your system) and open the editor in a browser.
